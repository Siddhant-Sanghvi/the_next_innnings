The Next Innings

Giving every kid on the maidan a chance to play.

Founded by Siddhant Sanghvi, Mumbai

My story

I’ve played cricket for over twelve years, from local maidans to the Cricket Club of India, at the interschool and inter-club level. The game taught me persistence, perseverance and humility. Batting at 11:30 in Mumbai’s heat is a test in itself.

On those same maidans I kept seeing something else: kids who loved cricket as much as I did, playing without pads, helmets, a proper bat, or any coaching. The only difference between them and me was access.

Our mission

The Next Innings collects unused cricket equipment and puts it in the hands of children across Mumbai who have the talent and passion but not the gear, including the children of the groundsmen who maintain the grounds we play on.
